## 2. Legal framework

Processing is currently governed by **Law No. 19,628 on the Protection of Private Life**, to the extent applicable. The reform introduced by **Law No. 21,719 (Chile's new Personal Data Protection Law)** generally enters into force on **1 December 2026**, according to its [official publication in the Library of the National Congress](https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/Navegar/imprimir?idNorma=1209272).

We will update this policy and our procedures in line with the applicable regulations. The use of Consenty does not in itself constitute a certification of compliance. Where we process data of individuals located in other jurisdictions, we will additionally take into account the regulations that apply there.

## 3. Data we process and where it comes from

### 3.1 Contact form

When you complete the form on the website, we collect the data you provide yourself:

- **First and last name** (required)
- **Email address** (required when Consenty is used; you may enter it in the form or in the widget)
- Phone number
- Company
- Job title
- Industry
- **Reason for contact** (required)
- **Message or description of the challenge** (required)

Fields not marked as required are optional; you may leave them blank without preventing submission. When Consenty is used, the phone number does not replace email verification. In the alternative form without Consenty, at least an email address or a phone number is needed.

When the form uses Consenty, submission additionally requires a declared RUT (Chilean national ID number) and verification of the email address through a one-time code. Consenty reuses the fields already entered and requests any that are missing. This verification demonstrates access to the email account; it does not by itself verify ownership of the RUT.

Consenty retains the identity and the evidence of privacy decisions, including purpose, version and time of acceptance. The content of the enquiry is delivered to our management systems and is not stored as a form response in Consenty. Consenty's services are hosted on AWS in the United States.

Do not include sensitive information or third-party data without authorization. If you prefer not to use Consenty or the form does not work, you can write directly to [contacto@japs.ing](mailto:contacto@japs.ing).

### 3.2 Direct communications

If you write to us by email, WhatsApp or social media, we process the identification and contact data contained in that communication, together with its content.

### 3.3 Data in the context of engineering services

In the course of delivering projects, we may access contact data of the client's counterparts, as well as technical or operational information that may incidentally contain personal data. Such data is processed in accordance with the terms of the relevant contract.

### 3.4 Account data in digital products

In our digital products, we process the data necessary to create and manage accounts, authenticate users and log their activity for security and traceability purposes.

### 3.5 Technical browsing data

Our hosting provider records technical data inherent to the operation of the internet — IP address, browser type, date and time of the request — in server logs, for the purpose of operating the service and safeguarding its security.

## 4. Cookies, security and absence of commercial tracking

This website does not use advertising pixels, heat maps or advertising scripts. We do not use your browsing to build commercial profiles or to track your activity across websites.

The website's delivery provider, **Cloudflare**, includes technical performance measurements (**RUM / Web Analytics**) to assess page load and operation. This service is separate from Turnstile. You can consult its [documentation on the data collected](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/data-metrics/data-origin-and-collection/). We do not use these metrics to send you advertising or as the content of a contact request.

To protect the form against automated submissions, we use **Cloudflare Turnstile**. This service runs technical checks in the browser to distinguish people from automated systems and may process technical data strictly necessary for that purpose, such as browser characteristics, IP address and the result of the challenge. Turnstile does not receive the fields or the content you type into the form. You can consult the [Turnstile privacy addendum](https://www.cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy/).

We do not enable Turnstile's pre-clearance feature, so the website does not ask this service to set an authorization cookie. If this configuration changes, we will update this policy before enabling it.

Links to external platforms — WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram and our products — take you to third-party websites that may use cookies in accordance with their own policies, which we recommend you review.

If in the future we incorporate any technology of this kind, we will update this policy and obtain the consent required by law before using it.

We process your data solely for the purposes indicated below, each with its legal basis:

| Purpose | Legal basis |

| Managing accounts and access in digital products | Performance of a contract |

## 6. Consent and its withdrawal

Where the legal basis is consent, we ask for an express and informed decision. When the form uses Consenty, you can read the purpose and the text in the widget, grant your authorization and click "Save consents" («Guardar consentimientos»). In the alternative form without Consenty, an acceptance checkbox is presented that is not pre-ticked. Authorization is not granted automatically by opening the form or by verifying your email address. The purpose is to respond to and follow up on your request, not to send you unsolicited advertising.

You may **withdraw your consent at any time**, without giving reasons, by writing to [contacto@japs.ing](mailto:contacto@japs.ing). Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out beforehand, nor does it prevent us from retaining data we are required to keep under a legal obligation.

If you gave your consent through Consenty, you can also review your decisions, withdraw them and submit data subject rights requests from the [Consenty portal](https://app.consenty.japs.ing/portal). Turnstile protection may run invisibly within this widget.

## 7. Retention periods

We retain data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose that justified its collection:

| Category | Retention period |
| --- | --- |
| Enquiries that do not lead to a business relationship | Up to 12 months from the last contact |
| Submission records for this form in Consenty | 12 months from issuance, kept separate from the content of the enquiry |
| Contact data of active clients and prospects | For as long as the business relationship continues |
| Contractual and project documentation | For the term of the contract and the limitation period for claims arising from it |
| Tax and accounting records | The applicable statutory periods |
| Account data in digital products | While the account is active, and thereafter as agreed with the client |
| Technical server logs | Limited periods defined by the hosting provider |

We arrange deletion or anonymization once these periods expire, unless a legal obligation or a specific claim justifies retaining certain records for longer. In that case, their use is limited to that need.

For new records/receipts generated by this form, Consenty's technical configuration prevents the validated evidence from being altered or deleted for a minimum of **365 days**. This is an integrity safeguard, not an automatic deletion task nor the retention period for all categories of data. Reviewing expiry dates and deleting data require operational management; changing the configuration does not modify earlier records.

The form's retention period does not automatically delete your Consenty account, the records of other organizations or the files of data subject rights requests. These are managed separately according to their purpose and the applicable obligations. You can check their status or request deletion through the channels indicated in this policy.

## 10. Our role in digital products

Two distinct roles coexist in our digital products, and it is important to distinguish between them:

- With respect to **account data** — user identification, credentials, access logs and billing — we act as **data controller**, and this policy applies in full.
- With respect to **data that the client uploads to the platform**, we act as **data processor**: the data controller is the client, who determines the purposes and means. We process that data solely in accordance with the client's documented instructions and the terms of the applicable data processing agreement.

### 10.1 Consenty

Consenty is a consent management platform developed in collaboration with Lumisreg Consulting. The consent records and associated evidence that the client manages on the platform belong to the client, who is their data controller. The Company processes them as a data processor, does not use them for its own purposes and makes them available to the client in accordance with the contract.

If you are a data subject whose data has been recorded in Consenty by a client organization, you must exercise your rights **before that organization**. If you contact us directly, we will forward your request to the data controller and inform you accordingly.

## 11. Security measures

We apply technical and organizational measures to protect data against loss, misuse, alteration and unauthorized access: encryption in transit, access control based on the principle of least privilege, user authentication, backups, activity logging and confidentiality duties for those who access the information.

No system is completely invulnerable. We are committed to keeping these measures up to date in line with the state of the art and the nature of the data processed.

## 12. Your rights

As a data subject, you have the right to:

- **Access**: know what data of yours we process, its source and its purpose.
- **Rectification**: obtain the correction of inaccurate, outdated or incomplete data.
- **Cancellation or erasure**: request deletion when the data is no longer necessary, you have withdrawn your consent or the processing lacks a legal basis.
- **Objection**: object to specific processing in the cases provided for by law.
- **Portability**: receive your data in a structured, commonly used format and request its transfer to another data controller where technically feasible.
- **Blocking**: request the temporary suspension of processing while a request is being resolved.

To exercise them, write to [contacto@japs.ing](mailto:contacto@japs.ing) stating your name, the right you are exercising and a copy of your national identity card or other means of proving your identity. We will respond within the statutory time limits. Exercising these rights is **free of charge**.

## 13. Complaints to the authority

If you believe your request was not handled correctly, you may turn to the competent bodies or courts under the regulations in force. The mechanisms before the **Personal Data Protection Agency** established by Law No. 21,719 will apply in accordance with its entry into force and the corresponding procedures.

## 14. Data of minors

Our services are aimed at companies and professionals, and we do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we become aware that we have received data from a minor without the authorization required by law, we will delete it.

## 15. Security breaches

In the event of a security incident affecting personal data, we will assess its scope and notify the authorities and affected data subjects where appropriate, within the time limits and under the conditions required by the regulations in force.

For data we process as a data processor, we will notify the client acting as data controller without delay so that it can fulfil its own obligations.
